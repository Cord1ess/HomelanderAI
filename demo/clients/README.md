# Demo clients

Five folders, one client each, ordered low risk to elevated. Drag a whole
folder into the intake form: `client.json` fills the client and the cover,
`blood-panel.json` fills the lab reader, and every other file is identified
and sent to its reader. `client.txt` is the same detail written out, for
reading or for typing in by hand.
Regenerate with `python scripts/make_demo_clients.py`.
